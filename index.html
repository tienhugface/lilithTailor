Getting started

Choose a wardrobe category and tap a garment to dress the mannequin. Use Canvas layers to select, adjust, hide, reorder or remove worn garments.

Turn on Advanced mode using the switch beside the sliders icon in the top navigation bar to access garment design and import, photo customisation, backgrounds, audio and export tools.

Common questions

How do I save a finished look?

In Advanced mode, open the export, import and share menu at the top right. Export a PNG or JPG to Files, or share it using the system share sheet. Export your finished look before closing the app.

What stays saved between sessions?

Saved wardrobe items, garment fitting settings, face customisation and preferences remain available. The current outfit composition, full-doll replacement, background, canvas drawing and audio reset when the app closes. A garment collection backup contains saved garments and their fitting settings; it does not save the current canvas.

How do I back up my wardrobe?

In Advanced mode, use the top-right menu to export your garment collection to a file. Import that file through the same menu to merge its garments into your library. Keep the backup somewhere you can access later.

Why does background removal keep part of a person?

Automatic removal detects the foreground subject and may include a wearer or shoulders. Use the erase, restore and tracing tools to refine your garment cutout. On iOS 16, a prepared transparent garment image may give better results.

How do I remove a saved garment?

In Advanced mode, open its wardrobe category, touch and hold its thumbnail, then choose Remove from wardrobe.

Do I need an account?

No account is required. Photo editing and background removal happen on your device.

Which devices are supported?

Lilith Tailor supports iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later. Apple Pencil drawing is available on compatible iPads.
