Privacy policy

Effective date: 2 Nov 2026

Tien Dao provides Lilith Tailor. For privacy questions, contact daotiend@gmail.com.

Information handling

Lilith Tailor does not require an account. We do not collect or receive your personal information through the app. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Photos and face processing

You can choose photos for garment designs, backgrounds, a replacement face or a replacement doll. Images are processed on your device. On-device face detection helps position a replacement face and estimate a mannequin skin colour. The app does not use this processing to identify you, create an identity profile or authenticate you. Images and face-processing results are not sent to us or third-party servers by the app.

Saved face images, an original image backup and fitting settings may remain in the app’s local storage so you can adjust or disable the replacement later. Switching the replacement face off does not delete these saved files.

Local storage and deletion

Saved garments, garment images, categories, fitting settings, face customisation and preferences are stored locally. The current canvas composition, full-doll replacement, background, drawing and imported audio are temporary and reset when the app closes. Local app data may be included in device backups according to your Apple and device settings.

You can remove saved wardrobe items through the app in Advanced mode. Deleting the app removes its local app data from that device. Exported files and device backups must be managed separately in their respective locations.

Files, audio and sharing

You may select files or audio for use in the app. When you export a look or garment collection, you choose where to save or share it using system tools. Exported collections include garment images, names, categories and fitting settings. Services or apps you choose for sharing handle received files under their own privacy policies.

Support enquiries

If you contact us directly, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and support message. We use that information to respond to your request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

Contact

For questions about this policy or a privacy request, email daotiend@gmail.com. You can also visit our support page.
